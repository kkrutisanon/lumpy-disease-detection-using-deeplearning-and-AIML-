About Us

Welcome to Cattle-Care AI, your trusted partner in safeguarding cattle health. Our innovative web application is designed to empower farmers and veterinarians with the tools they need to prevent and mitigate the devastating effects of Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD). Leveraging advanced machine learning technology, we provide accurate and timely disease detection, helping to protect livestock and ensure sustainable agricultural practices.

Our Aim

Our aim is to revolutionize Lumpy Skin Disease prevention by offering a user-friendly, affordable, and highly accurate AI-powered solution. By empowering farmers and veterinarians with early detection capabilities, we strive to minimize the economic impact of LSD outbreaks and promote the overall well-being of cattle populations.

Our Objective

Our objective is to develop and deploy a cutting-edge Lumpy Skin Disease detection tool that is accessible to farmers and veterinarians worldwide. By harnessing the power of artificial intelligence, we aim to improve disease surveillance, facilitate rapid response efforts, and contribute to the overall health and productivity of cattle herds.

Our Team

Our dedicated team of experts brings together a diverse range of skills and experience in computer science, veterinary medicine, and agricultural technology. Passionate about animal health and innovation, we are committed to developing and delivering solutions that make a positive impact on the livestock industry.

Our Technology

Our Lumpy Skin Disease detection model is built on a robust foundation of deep learning architectures, including Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Residual Networks (ResNets), and EfficientNets. These advanced models are trained on a meticulously curated and preprocessed dataset sourced from authentic and reliable sources, ensuring the accuracy and reliability of our predictions.
